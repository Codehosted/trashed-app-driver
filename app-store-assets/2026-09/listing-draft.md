## Description

One Trashed app for vendors and drivers.

Keep your business moving from the office to the jobsite. Sign in with your
existing Trashed account to open the tools available to your role.

FOR VENDORS
Access your Trashed workspace for orders, customers, inventory, dispatch, and
business calls. Your mobile workspace stays connected to the website.

FOR DRIVERS
Open assigned routes, review stops, share progress with dispatch, and manage
on-the-job updates. Location sharing starts when you choose to go online and
grant permission, and stops when you go offline.

STAY INFORMED
Receive work updates for business call results, orders needing approval, and
route changes. Review real notifications in your inbox. Notifications require
your permission and depend on your account's access and enabled services.

CALL WITH YOUR PHONE
Customer phone links open your device's phone app. You stay in control of
whether to place the call. Standard carrier charges may apply.

Made for authorized Trashed vendor teams and drivers. Features depend on your
role and account access. Background location is used only for enabled driver
tracking, with permission.

## Proposed release notes

- One app for vendors and drivers, with role-aware access to your workspace.
- A first-launch walkthrough and a splash screen that does not replay between screens.
- Clearer app artwork and safer placement of mobile back and close controls.
- Real work notifications and direct links to the relevant details.
- Customer call links use your phone's native dialer.
